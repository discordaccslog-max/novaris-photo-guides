What's inside

Contents of the guide

The guide is organized into the sections below. Every section is included in both the US Letter and A4 files.

Tabletop lighting setups

Top-down diagrams of tabletop lighting arrangements, showing light position, modifier type, bounce cards, and background placement relative to the product.

Background and surface reference

Reference pages describing common backgrounds and surfaces, such as seamless sweeps, textured boards, and reflective surfaces, and how each tends to behave under light.

Shot lists for e-commerce angles

Checklists covering standard catalogue angles, including front, three-quarter, side, back, top-down, detail, and scale shots, so each product gets a consistent set.

Settings charts for common scenarios

Charts with starting-point settings for situations such as white backgrounds, reflective items, and flat lays, with space to record your own adjustments.

Representative pages

Illustrations of three representative page layouts from this guide. They show the layout and structure of each page type.

Fit

Who it's for, and who it isn't

A good fit for

  • Photographers shooting products on a tabletop for online shops, catalogues, or listings
  • Small-business owners who photograph their own products and want a consistent shot list for every item
  • Anyone who wants a printed lighting and settings reference beside the shooting table

Not the right fit for

  • Anyone looking for a course or step-by-step lessons: this guide is reference material, not instruction
  • Large-product, vehicle, or architectural photography that doesn't fit on a tabletop
  • Anyone who needs editing or retouching instructions: the guide covers setup and capture planning only
  • Anyone who needs editable templates: the PDFs are flat and designed for printing
