Support email

buckenovaris@novarisguides.com

Response time: within two business days.

What to include

So we can find your order and answer in one reply, please include:

  • Your order number, from your confirmation page or the payment provider's receipt.
  • The email address you used at checkout.
  • The guide your message is about.
  • What you need, such as a replacement license code, help with the Field Kit, or a refund.

Refunds

To request a refund, email us within 14 days of purchase with your order number. No reason is required. See the Refund Policy.

Lost license codes

Email us with your order number and the email used at checkout, and we'll send a replacement code. If you activated the code on any device, it's also listed in the Field Kit's settings under Licenses.

Problems with the Field Kit

The Report a problem link at the bottom of each Field Kit section opens an email to us with the guide and section filled in. Tell us what happened and what device and browser you use.

Privacy requests

To access, correct, or delete the order information we hold, or to object to how we use it, email us. See the Privacy Policy.

Before you write

Many questions about licenses, devices, offline use, and backups are answered on the FAQ page. The guides are reference tools, so we don't offer critique of photographs or individual photography advice by email.
