What to include

To help us find your order and answer in one reply, please include:

  • Your order number, from your confirmation email or the order confirmation screen.
  • The email address you used at checkout, if you're writing from a different address.
  • The guide your question is about.
  • A short description of what you need, such as a resent download link, a replacement file, or a refund.

Common requests

  • Download email didn't arrive: check your spam folder first. If it's not there after an hour, email us and we'll resend the link. See the Delivery Policy.
  • Refund: send your order number within 14 days of purchase. No reason is required. See the Refund Policy.
  • File won't open: tell us what happens when you open it, and we'll send a replacement or a refund, whichever you prefer.
  • Privacy requests: to access, correct, or delete your information, see the Privacy Policy.

Before you write

Many questions about formats, printing, and licensing are answered on the FAQ page. Please note that the guides are reference material, and we don't offer critique of photographs or individual photography advice by email.
