What's inside

Contents of the guide

The guide is organized into the sections below. Every section is included in both the US Letter and A4 files.

Shot lists by event type

Separate shot lists for corporate events, parties, and conferences, covering venue and signage, speakers, attendees, key moments, and details.

Low-light settings reference

Charts with starting-point settings for dim venues, stage lighting, mixed light, and evening receptions, with space to note your adjustments.

Flash setups

Diagrams of on-camera bounce flash and simple off-camera flash arrangements for rooms of different sizes, showing flash direction and bounce surfaces.

Run-of-show planning sheets

Sheets for matching coverage to the event schedule: time, segment, location, people involved, and shots that must not be missed.

Page previews

Representative pages

Illustrations of three representative page layouts from this guide. They show the layout and structure of each page type.

Fit

Who it's for, and who it isn't

A good fit for

  • Photographers covering corporate events, conferences, launches, and private parties
  • Photographers who work from a client's schedule and want coverage planned against it
  • Anyone who wants a printed low-light and flash reference for dim venues

Not the right fit for

  • Anyone looking for event photography lessons: this guide is reference and planning material, not instruction
  • Weddings: the Wedding Day Guide is built for that day's structure
  • Concert or sports coverage with its own access rules and equipment demands
  • Anyone who needs editable templates: the PDFs are flat and meant for printing
