Who you are buying from

You are buying from Novaris Photo Guides. You can reach us by email at buckenovaris@novarisguides.com or through our Contact page. Payment is taken by a third-party payment provider at checkout.

What you are buying

Each purchase is a license to digital content: a browser-based field guide in the Novaris Field Kit, unlocked by a license code. It is supplied immediately after payment on the order confirmation page. Each guide's page describes its contents and functionality and lists what you need to use it (a modern web browser on a phone, tablet, or computer).

Price and taxes

Prices are shown in US dollars. Applicable taxes, such as VAT or sales tax, are calculated for your location and shown at checkout before you pay, together with the total you will be charged.

Your right to cancel and our refund policy

For digital content, EU and UK law gives consumers a 14-day right to cancel that normally ends once supply begins, if you agreed to that. You don't need to rely on that rule with us: our Refund Policy gives you a full refund on any purchase if you ask within 14 days of purchase, with no reason required, even though your guide is supplied straight away. To use it, contact us with your order number. Refunds go to your original payment method within 5 business days of your request, and your license ends.

If the content is faulty or not as described

Under EU and UK consumer law, digital content must match its description, be fit for purpose, and be of satisfactory quality. If a guide doesn't work as described, tell us through the Contact page. We'll work to fix the problem; if we can't, you're entitled to a refund. These statutory rights apply in addition to our Refund Policy and are not limited by our Terms of Service.

Using the content

The guides work in current versions of common browsers on phones, tablets, and computers. Your saved work is stored in your own browser, so please keep a backup with the Field Kit's Export backup feature. See the Delivery Policy for how access works across devices and offline.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with anything, email buckenovaris@novarisguides.com. We reply within two business days and aim to resolve complaints directly.

Customers elsewhere

Wherever you live, our 14-day Refund Policy applies, together with any rights you have under the consumer protection laws where you live.
