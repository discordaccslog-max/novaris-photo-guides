Wedding Day Guide

An interactive field guide for covering a wedding day from preparation to reception, built for use on a phone. It holds a full-day shot list organized by phase, an editable wedding timeline, a family formals grouping planner, a venue scouting worksheet, a gear checklist, a client questionnaire, and a settings reference with an exposure calculator.

An interactive field guide that runs in the Novaris Field Kit in your web browser and unlocks with a license code. It is a reference and planning tool for use while you work, not a course, lesson, or critique service.

What's inside

What the guide contains and does

Everything below is in the guide. Your saved work stays on your device, and every list, worksheet, and diagram can be printed or exported as a PDF.

Full-day shot list by phase

Preparation, ceremony, formals, and reception in shooting order. Tick shots off during the day, follow the progress bar and the next shot, and customize the list for each couple. Saved per wedding on your device.

Full-day timeline

An editable timeline with time, event, location, and notes for each part of the day. Add, move, or remove rows, tick them off on the day, and export a PDF to share with the couple or planner.

Family formals grouping planner

List each group in shooting order with the names of everyone in it, then tick groups off as you photograph them.

Venue scouting worksheet

Record ceremony and reception spaces, light at the right times, ceiling height for bounce flash, portrait spots, and bad-weather backups.

Gear checklist

A packing and checking list by category, written without reference to any brand, that you can tick off and customize.

Client questionnaire and settings

A questionnaire you edit and export as a PDF for the couple, plus wedding settings starting points and an exposure calculator.

Live previews

Try parts of the guide

These previews run the real Field Kit. Tap, tick, and change values to see how it works. Nothing you do here is saved.

Fit

Who it's for, and who it isn't

A good fit for

  • Photographers covering full wedding days who plan timelines and shot lists in advance
  • Second shooters and assistants who need the plan on their phone on the day
  • Photographers who want the same questionnaire and checklist for every wedding

Not the right fit for

  • Anyone looking for a wedding photography course: this is a planning and reference tool, not lessons
  • Wedding planners looking for vendor or budget management
  • Anyone looking for posing or lighting diagrams: this guide focuses on planning and shot lists
