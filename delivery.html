All products are digital and delivered instantly. Field Kit guides are delivered as a license code; PDF guides and their add-ons are delivered by download. There is no physical shipment.

Field Kit guides: how delivery works

  1. Add to cart and pay. Add the guide to your cart. In your cart, you tick that you agree to the Terms of Service and Refund Policy, then pay at checkout.
  2. Confirmation page. As soon as payment is complete, you're shown a confirmation page. It states that your order is complete, shows your product, the date and time, your order reference when the payment provider supplies one, and your license code, and it activates the license in that browser automatically. You can save the confirmation as a PDF or print it.
  3. Open your guide. The Open Field Kit button takes you straight into your guide in the Novaris Field Kit.
  4. Other devices. To use your guide on another phone, tablet, or computer, open the Field Kit there and enter the same license code.

PDF guides: how delivery works

  1. Choose and pay. On the guide's page, tick any add-ons you want and add the guide to your cart. In your cart, tick that you agree to the Terms of Service and Refund Policy, then pay at checkout.
  2. Download. As soon as payment is complete, your download link is shown on the checkout confirmation and sent to the email address you entered at checkout. It includes the guide and every add-on you chose.
  3. Keep your files. Save the PDFs to your devices. They open in any PDF viewer and work without a connection.

If your download email hasn't arrived within an hour, check your spam folder, then contact us through the Contact page with your order number and we'll send your files again.

Offline use

Once the Field Kit has loaded on a device while online, you can install it to the home screen and use your guides offline on location.

Lost license codes

If you lose your code, request a replacement through our Contact page with your order number and the email used at checkout. If you activated the code on any device, it's also listed in the Field Kit's settings under Licenses.

How long access lasts

Online access to the Field Kit continues for as long as Novaris Photo Guides operates. Your saved work is stored on your own devices, so please keep a backup using the Field Kit's Export backup feature.

No physical shipment

Nothing is printed or posted, and there are no shipping charges.

Questions

Use our Contact page. We reply within two business days.
