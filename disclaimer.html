Reference and planning tools, not instruction

Novaris Photo Guides are reference and planning tools: shot lists, diagrams, calculators, and worksheets for photographers to use while planning and working. They are not courses, lessons, or coaching, and they include no feedback on anyone's work.

Settings and setups are starting points

Gear, light, venues, and subjects all vary. The settings, lighting setups, diagrams, and calculator results in the guides are starting points for you to adjust to your situation. They are not guaranteed to produce any particular result, and you remain responsible for the choices you make on a shoot.

Sun times are for reference

Sunrise, sunset, golden hour, and blue hour times are calculated in your browser for reference. Terrain, buildings, weather, and the time zone you choose all affect when light actually reaches you. Allow a margin when planning.

Your responsibilities when photographing

When you use the guides, you are responsible for:

  • obtaining permits or permission where required, including for parks, venues, and public spaces;
  • respecting private property and site rules;
  • getting model releases and property releases where they are needed;
  • following local laws on photographing people and places;
  • using equipment safely, including light stands, cables, and flash around people.

Independence

Novaris Photo Guides is independent. We are not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any camera, lens, lighting, or software maker. The guides describe equipment by type and do not recommend any brand.
