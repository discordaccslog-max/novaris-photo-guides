The short version: this website uses no cookies, no analytics, and no advertising trackers. Your licenses, saved shot lists, worksheets, and any location you choose to use are stored only in your own browser and are never sent to us. When you buy, we receive basic order information from the payment provider and use it only for support, refunds, and tax and accounting. We never sell it or share it for advertising.

Who we are

This policy covers the Novaris Photo Guides website, the Novaris Field Kit, and our store. For any privacy question or request, use our Contact page.

What this website collects

This website and the Field Kit use no cookies, no analytics, no advertising trackers, and no forms that send information to us. Our fonts are served from this website itself.

The website is hosted by Vercel. To deliver pages and keep the service secure, Vercel processes the technical information that every web request carries, such as your IP address, browser type, and the page requested, under its own privacy policy. We don't use this information to identify or profile you.

What stays on your device

The Field Kit stores the following only in your browser's storage on the device you're using:

  • license codes you activate;
  • shot lists, checklists, worksheets, timelines, planners, and questionnaires you create;
  • display and export preferences;
  • any location you save in the sun planner.

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. We can't see it, and we can't recover it if your browser or device deletes it, so please use the Export backup feature. Backup files are saved to your device and stay under your control.

Location. The sun planner can use your current location if you choose "Use my current location" and allow it in your browser. The coordinates are used only on your device to calculate sun times, and are stored only in your browser if you save them. You can enter coordinates by hand instead.

Payments

Payments are handled by third-party payment providers. At checkout, the payment provider collects your name, email address, billing details, and payment information and processes them under its own privacy policy. Novaris Photo Guides never sees your full payment card details.

Information we receive when you buy

After a purchase, we receive order information from the payment provider: your name, your email address, the products you purchased, and your country. If you email us, we also receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

How we use it

We use order information only to provide support (including replacing lost license codes), to process refunds, and to meet our tax and accounting obligations. For visitors in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, our legal bases are the performance of our contract with you and compliance with legal obligations.

What we never do

We never sell personal information, and we never share it for advertising, including cross-context behavioral advertising. We share order information only with the payment provider that processes your payment, and otherwise only where needed to meet tax and accounting obligations or where required by law.

How long we keep information

We keep order information for as long as tax and accounting law requires, and then delete it. Support emails are kept while needed to resolve your request and, where they relate to an order or refund, for the same record-keeping periods.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it (except where we must keep it for legal, tax, or accounting reasons), and you can object to or ask us to restrict how we use it. Make a request through our Contact page, ideally from the email address you used at checkout. We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

Residents of the EEA and UK (GDPR)

Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR you have the rights above, plus the right to data portability. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. We respond to requests within one month.

California residents (CCPA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended, you have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, and the rights to access, correct, and delete it. We don't sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of. We won't treat you differently for exercising your rights. We respond to requests within 45 days.

Children

This website and the guides are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the updated version on this page and change the "Last updated" date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use order information we already hold, we'll also email the customers it affects before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: use our Contact page.
