About Novaris Photo Guides

We make interactive field guides for photographers. Each one gathers the shot lists, diagrams, calculators, and worksheets for one kind of photography into a tool you can use on your phone during a shoot.

What we make

Novaris Photo Guides sells five field guides, each covering one kind of photography, and a library that unlocks all five with one license. The guides cover portrait sessions, wedding days, product photography, landscape and travel, and event coverage.

The guides run in the Novaris Field Kit, a web app that works in any modern browser on a phone, tablet, or computer. Inside are the things a photographer reaches for while planning or working: shot lists you can tick off, lighting diagrams you can tap to see what each light does, posing references, settings tables with an exposure calculator, a sunrise and sunset planner, and planning worksheets. Which of these a guide contains depends on the kind of photography; each guide's page lists exactly what's inside.

Why a field guide on your phone

Much of the work around a shoot is remembering things: which shots a client asked for, where a light went last time, when the sun drops behind the ridge, which family group comes next. That information tends to live in scattered notes and screenshots.

A phone is already in your pocket on every shoot. The Field Kit puts the plan and the reference on it, in a layout made for quick glances: large buttons, a progress bar on every shot list, a high-contrast mode for bright sun, and a red night mode for dark venues. When paper is better, every worksheet and list prints or exports as a PDF.

How the guides are built

  • One kind of photography per guide, so each stays focused.
  • Your work stays on your device. Saved shot lists, worksheets, and locations are stored only in your browser. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Works offline. Install the Field Kit to your home screen and your guides work without a signal once loaded.
  • Brand-neutral. Equipment is described by type, such as a softbox or a reflector, never by manufacturer.
  • Starting points, not rules. Settings and setups are places to begin. Light, gear, and subjects vary, and you adjust from there.

What the guides are not

The guides are reference and planning tools. They aren't courses, video lessons, coaching, or critique, and they don't include feedback on anyone's photographs. The Disclaimer covers this in more detail.

How we sell

Prices are listed in US dollars on each guide's page, and applicable taxes are shown at checkout. Payment is handled by a third-party payment provider, so we never see your full card details. Your license code appears on your confirmation page as soon as payment is complete. If a guide isn't right for you, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days, with no reason required.

This website uses no cookies, no analytics, and no advertising trackers. The Privacy Policy explains what information we receive when you buy and how we use it.
