Studio Lighting Setups Library

Eight overhead studio lighting diagrams with placement notes for one to four lights, plus light ratio and modifier references.

This library collects classic studio setups, from a single light to four, each drawn from above with notes for every light and modifier. Use it to set up quickly, reproduce a look, or plan a shoot.

Distances and angles are starting points. Watch where shadows fall on the face and adjust from there.

Loop lighting: one light

Key 30–45° to the side, slightly above. A small loop-shaped shadow beside the nose.

Lighting setup

Rembrandt lighting

Key at about 45° and high enough to make a small triangle of light on the shadow cheek.

Lighting setup

Split lighting

Key at 90° lights exactly half the face, for strong mood.

Lighting setup

Butterfly or clamshell lighting

Key centered above the camera, with a reflector below. A butterfly-shaped shadow under the nose.

Lighting setup

Key and fill

A soft fill near the camera controls contrast.

Lighting setup

Key and rim

A rim light behind separates the subject from a dark background.

Three-point lighting

Key, fill, and a background light: the standard balanced setup.

High-key white background

Two lights on a white backdrop make it pure white; a key lights the subject.
