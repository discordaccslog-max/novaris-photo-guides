What you get with each guide

Every guide follows the same format, so the pages work the same way whichever guide you open.

Two page sizes

Each guide comes as a US Letter PDF and an A4 PDF, so it prints at full size on standard paper in North America and elsewhere.

Flat, print-ready PDFs

The files are flat PDFs laid out for printing. They aren't editable or fillable; print the pages you need and write on them.

Reference pages, not lessons

Shot lists, diagrams, charts, and worksheets to consult during planning and on the job. No video, no course modules, no critique.

Brand-neutral throughout

Gear checklists and setups describe equipment by type, such as a softbox or a reflector, never by manufacturer or model.

Use in your own work

Use the guides in personal and commercial photography, print them as often as you need for your own use, and share client-facing worksheets with your clients.

Simple refunds

Ask for a full refund within 14 days of purchase, with no reason required and nothing to return.
