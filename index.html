04How the Field Kit works

How the Field Kit guides work

Every interactive guide runs in the same Field Kit, so they all work the same way.

Runs in your browser

Works in any modern browser on a phone, tablet, or computer. There's nothing to download from an app store.

One license, any device

Your license code unlocks your guide. Enter the same code on each phone, tablet, or computer you use.

Offline on location

Install the Field Kit to your home screen and, once loaded, your guides work without a signal.

Saved on your device

Shot lists, worksheets, and locations are stored only in your browser and never sent anywhere. Backups are built in.

Print or save as PDF

Every worksheet, shot list, and diagram can be printed or exported as a PDF in US Letter or A4.

Built for the field

Large touch targets, a high-contrast outdoor mode for bright sun, and a dim red night mode for dark venues.

05Purchase and delivery

How buying a guide works

Everything is digital and delivered instantly. There is no account to create and nothing is shipped.

  1. Review the guide

    Each guide's page shows what it contains, live previews, the price, and who it is and isn't for.

  2. Add to cart and check out

    Add guides to your cart, with any add-ons, confirm that you agree to the Terms of Service and Refund Policy, then pay at checkout. Applicable taxes are shown at checkout.

  3. Get your guide

    Field Kit guides: your confirmation page shows your license code and activates it. PDF guides: your download link, with any add-ons, is shown right after payment and emailed to you.

  4. Use it anywhere

    Enter your Field Kit code on your other devices, or save and print your PDFs. Both work on location without a signal.

Read the Delivery Policy and Refund Policy for the details.

06Questions

Common questions

What do I get when I buy a guide?

Instant access. After payment you're taken to a confirmation page that shows your license code and activates it in that browser, with an Open Field Kit button that goes straight into your guide. See the Delivery Policy.

What's the difference between Field Kit and PDF guides?

Field Kit guides are interactive: they run in your browser, save your shot lists and worksheets, and unlock with a license code. PDF guides are printable files you download after payment, with optional add-ons such as pocket cards, planners, and templates.

Do the guides work on my phone, and offline?

Yes. The Field Kit runs in any modern web browser on a phone, tablet, or computer. Install it to your home screen and, once it has loaded, your guides work offline on location.

Can I use one license on more than one device?

Yes. Open the Field Kit on the other device and enter the same license code. A Complete Reference Library code unlocks all five guides.

What if I change my mind?

You can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase, with no reason required, through the Contact page. See the Refund Policy.
