What's inside

The five guides in the library

The library contains each of these guides in full, exactly as they are sold individually.

Portrait Session Guide

Shot lists by session type, posing reference diagrams, natural-light and one- or two-light setups, and client session planning sheets.

Wedding Day Guide

Full-day timeline templates, shot lists by phase, a family formals grouping planner, a venue scouting worksheet, a gear checklist, and a client questionnaire.

Product Photography Guide

Tabletop lighting setups, background and surface reference, shot lists for standard e-commerce angles, and settings charts for common scenarios.

Landscape & Travel Field Guide

Location scouting worksheets, sunrise and sunset planning sheets, composition reference, field settings charts, and a gear checklist, plus a pocket format for a camera bag.

Event Coverage Guide

Shot lists for corporate events, parties, and conferences, low-light settings reference, flash setups, and run-of-show planning sheets.

Fit

Who it's for, and who it isn't

A good fit for

  • Photographers who work across several kinds of photography and want the whole set of references
  • Anyone who wants one consistent set of shot lists, diagrams, charts, and worksheets for portraits, weddings, products, landscapes, and events

Not the right fit for

  • Anyone who only needs one kind of reference: each guide is also sold on its own
  • Anyone looking for a course or lessons: the library is reference and planning material, not instruction
  • Anyone who needs editable templates: every guide is a flat PDF meant for printing
