Buying and delivery

What do I receive after I buy, and how?

As soon as payment is complete, you're shown a confirmation page. It shows your product, the date and time, your order reference when the payment provider supplies one, and your license code. The code is activated automatically in that browser, and the Open Field Kit button takes you straight into your guide. You can save the confirmation as a PDF or print it. Nothing is shipped. See the Delivery Policy.

Who handles payment, and are taxes included?

Payment is handled at checkout by a third-party payment provider; Novaris Photo Guides never sees your full card details. Prices are shown in US dollars, and applicable taxes are calculated and shown at checkout before you pay.

Why do I have to tick a box before buying?

Before checkout you confirm that you agree to the Terms of Service and Refund Policy, so the license terms and the 14-day refund are clear before you pay.

What does the Complete Reference Library include?

All five guides: Portrait Session, Wedding Day, Product Photography, Landscape & Travel Field, and Event Coverage. One license code unlocks all of them in the Field Kit.

Licenses and devices

How do licenses work?

Each purchase comes with a license code that starts with NOVA. Entering it in the Field Kit unlocks your guide in that browser. Codes are checked on your device; there's no account or password. Capitals, spaces, and dashes don't matter when you type a code.

How do I use my guide on another device?

Open the Field Kit on the other phone, tablet, or computer and enter the same license code. Your licenses are saved separately on each device. To move saved shot lists and worksheets too, use Export backup and Import backup in the Field Kit's settings.

Which devices and browsers are supported?

Any phone, tablet, or computer with a modern browser, such as a current version of Safari, Chrome, Edge, or Firefox. The Field Kit is designed for phones first, with large touch targets, and switches to a sidebar layout on larger screens.

I've lost my license code. What now?

If you activated it on a device, the code is listed in the Field Kit's settings under Licenses. Otherwise, write to us through the Contact page with your order number and the email you used at checkout, and we'll send you a replacement.

Can I share my license code?

No. A license is for your own use. Codes may not be shared, published, sold, or transferred. See the Terms of Service.

Using the Field Kit

Does it work offline on location?

Yes. After the Field Kit has loaded once while you're online, install it to your home screen: in Safari, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen; in other browsers, choose Install app or Add to Home screen. Your guides and saved work then open without a signal.

Where is my work saved? Should I back it up?

Shot lists, worksheets, settings, and any saved location are stored only in your browser on that device and never sent anywhere. Browsers can clear stored data, for example when a device runs low on space or if you clear site data, so use Export backup in the Field Kit's settings regularly. The Field Kit reminds you if you haven't made a backup in 30 days.

Can I print or export worksheets?

Yes. Every shot list, worksheet, timeline, planner, diagram, and settings table has Export PDF and Print buttons. Choose US Letter or A4 in the Field Kit's settings. Worksheets can also be exported blank to fill in by hand.

Can I share the client questionnaires with my clients?

Yes. In the Portrait Session and Wedding Day guides you can edit the questions, add your business name and how clients should return it, and export a PDF to send to your own clients. Sharing questionnaires with your clients is part of your license.

How is my location used in the sun planner?

The sun planner calculates sunrise, sunset, golden hour, and blue hour on your device. If you choose Use my current location, your browser asks permission and the coordinates are used only on your device. If you save a location, it's stored only in your browser and is never sent to us or anyone else.

Are gear recommendations brand-specific?

No. Equipment is described by type, such as a softbox, reflector, flash, or tripod. No camera, lens, lighting, or software brand is recommended, and Novaris Photo Guides isn't affiliated with any manufacturer.

Are these courses, or will you review my photos?

Neither. The guides are reference and planning tools. They aren't lessons, and they don't include feedback or critique on anyone's work.

Refunds and support

What is the refund policy?

You can request a full refund on any purchase within 14 days, with no reason required, through the Contact page with your order number. Refunds go to your original payment method within 5 business days of your request; your payment provider may take a few more days to process it. Your license ends when the purchase is refunded. See the Refund Policy.

Something isn't working. What should I do?

Use the Report a problem link in the Field Kit, which opens an email to us with the guide and section filled in, or write to buckenovaris@novarisguides.com. We reply within two business days.
