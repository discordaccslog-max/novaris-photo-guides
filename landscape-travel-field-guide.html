What's inside

Contents of the guide

The guide is organized into the sections below. Every section is included in both the US Letter and A4 files.

Location scouting worksheets

Worksheets for recording a location before or during a visit: access, parking, permits, hazards, viewpoints, and a sketch area for marking positions.

Sunrise and sunset planning sheets

Sheets for noting sunrise, sunset, golden hour and blue hour times, sun direction on a compass diagram, weather, and when to arrive.

Composition reference

Diagram pages illustrating common composition structures, such as leading lines, foreground framing, layering, and horizon placement.

Field settings charts

Charts with starting-point settings for common field situations, such as handheld scenes, tripod work at low light, moving water, and night skies.

Gear checklist

A packing checklist for day trips and longer travel, organized by category and written without reference to any brand.

Pocket format

A pocket version of the field references, sized to carry in a camera bag for use on location.

Representative pages

Illustrations of three representative page layouts from this guide. They show the layout and structure of each page type.

Fit

Who it's for, and who it isn't

A good fit for

  • Photographers who plan landscape shoots around sunrise, sunset, and weather
  • Travelling photographers who want a printed scouting and packing routine
  • Anyone who prefers a paper reference in the camera bag over checking a phone on location

Not the right fit for

  • Anyone looking for a landscape photography course: this guide is reference and planning material, not instruction
  • Anyone who needs location-specific information, maps, or permit details for particular places
  • Anyone who needs fillable digital worksheets: the PDFs are flat and meant to be printed
  • Anyone looking for editing or post-processing guidance
