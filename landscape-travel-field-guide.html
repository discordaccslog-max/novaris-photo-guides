Landscape & Travel Field Guide

An interactive field guide for landscape and travel work, built for a phone on location. It calculates sunrise, sunset, golden hour, and blue hour for any date and place on your device, and holds location scouting worksheets, a composition reference, field settings with an exposure and ND filter calculator, a gear checklist, and a printable pocket card.

An interactive field guide that runs in the Novaris Field Kit in your web browser and unlocks with a license code. It is a reference and planning tool for use while you work, not a course, lesson, or critique service.

What's inside

What the guide contains and does

Everything below is in the guide. Your saved work stays on your device, and every list, worksheet, and diagram can be printed or exported as a PDF.

Sunrise and sunset planner

Sunrise, sunset, solar noon, golden hour, and blue hour times for any date and location, with sunrise and sunset directions on a compass. Use your current location if you like; coordinates are stored only on your device.

Location scouting worksheet

Record access, permits, best light, viewpoints, foreground interest, hazards, and phone signal for each location. Saved on your device and exportable as a PDF.

Composition reference

Diagrams of thirds, leading lines, foreground framing, layers, horizon placement, and symmetry, with a note on when each works.

Field settings and calculator

Starting points for handheld scenes, golden and blue hour, moving water, waves, night skies, and street scenes, plus a calculator that includes ND filters for long exposures.

Gear checklist

A packing list for day trips and travel that you can tick off and customize.

Pocket card

A 4 × 6 in printable card with field settings, sun times for your saved location, and your gear checklist, to carry in a camera bag.

Live previews

Try parts of the guide

These previews run the real Field Kit. Tap, tick, and change values to see how it works. Nothing you do here is saved.

Fit

Who it's for, and who it isn't

A good fit for

  • Photographers who plan landscape shoots around sunrise, sunset, and weather
  • Traveling photographers who want a scouting and packing routine on their phone
  • Anyone who wants sun times and settings available offline on location

Not the right fit for

  • Anyone looking for a landscape photography course: this is a reference and planning tool, not instruction
  • Anyone who needs maps, trail information, or permit details for particular places
  • Anyone looking for editing or post-processing guidance
Requirements

What you need

  • DeviceA phone, tablet, or computer with a modern web browser, such as a current version of Safari, Chrome, Edge, or Firefox
  • ConnectionInternet access to activate your license and load the Field Kit the first time; after installing, guides work offline
  • StorageYour saved work is kept in your browser's storage on each device; use the built-in backup to keep a copy
  • ExportsA PDF viewer or printer for the PDFs you export
  • GearAny camera: equipment is described by type, never by brand
  • LicensePersonal and commercial use in your own photography work. See the Terms of Service.

How delivery works

After payment you're shown a confirmation page with your license code, already activated in that browser. The Open Field Kit button takes you straight into the guide. To use it on another device, open the Field Kit there and enter the same code. Details are in the Delivery Policy.
